A large LF909 manual was discoverable but repeated full-document reads timed out; no new LF909 dimension or installation instruction was inferred from it.

The original schematic `installation-site-reference.svg` is a conceptual site brief, not a dimensional drawing. An open relief path and a receiving drain do not determine capacity, air-gap sizing, hazard acceptance or approval. The named manufacturer's complete instructions remain the authority.
