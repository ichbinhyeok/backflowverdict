Manufacturer PDFs were read through the web research reader, including part-table page boundaries and the 007 exception note. Some screenshot/direct fetches were blocked or timed out; those responses are not evidence of dead links. The older FEBCO 765 URL and the large 909 troubleshooting manual were not used for new published claims. The UMass HTML address was inaccessible, so the accessible official PDF is cited instead.

New SVGs are original conceptual observation maps, not copied product drawings, exploded assembly instructions, pressure schematics with complete test-port counts, or approved dimensional plans. The single-cover DCVA view explicitly warns against identifying an assembly from cover count. The PVB view has one check and an air inlet downstream of that check. Exact manufacturer documents control service.
