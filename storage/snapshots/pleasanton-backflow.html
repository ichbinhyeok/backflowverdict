Pleasanton cross-connection control snapshot

Captured for the file-backed registry on 2026-07-06 by reviewer TL.

Durable excerpt: Pleasanton's official cross-connection control plan says testers submit qualifications and completed reports through the City's third-party online portal, currently Aqua Backflow, and that testers can be removed for expired credentials, expired gauge calibration, expired business license, or failure to enter completed reports online.
