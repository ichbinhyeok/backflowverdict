# Public Source Fact Research - 2026-07-09

Purpose: add non-generic operational facts to already visible or high-intent backflow pages. These are public-source facts, not inferred SEO filler.

## Sources Reviewed

1. Aurora Water Backflow Prevention
   - URL: https://www.auroragov.org/residents/water/pay_my_water_bill/backflow_prevention
   - Useful facts: certified technician testing on installation and at least annually; online result submission at the Aurora Water backflow site before the annual due date; responsible party is the billed customer or property owner.
   - Site action: add SpryBackflow aliasing, official submission URL, SpryBackflow workflow language, and Aurora priority portal paths.

2. Hillsborough County Backflow and Cross Connection Service Testing
   - URL: https://hcfl.gov/residents/property-owners-and-renters/water-and-sewer/backflow-and-cross-connection-service-testing
   - Useful facts: annual testing for owners with assemblies; only registered county-certified testers; Backflow BMP platform; enrollment requires current testing certifications and test-kit calibration certifications; failed assemblies must be reported within 48 hours.
   - Site action: add Backflow BMP portal language, test-kit calibration proof, and 48-hour failed-test risk.

3. Anaheim Cross Connection Control
   - URL: https://www.anaheim.net/769/Cross-Connection-Control
   - Useful facts: annual tests must use Orange County-certified testers with a valid Anaheim business license and city approved-list status; mailed/email reports are no longer accepted; SwiftComply submission requires test report information and a photo of the assembly during the test.
   - Site action: add SwiftComply registration URL, photo evidence requirement, and rejection-risk copy.

4. Austin Water BPAT Information
   - URL: https://www.austintexas.gov/water/backflow-prevention-assembly-tester-information
   - Useful facts: BPATs must hold TCEQ license and Austin Water registration; TMRs go through WEIRS within five calendar days; TMR data includes address, cause, assembly identifiers, results, repair/retest details, owner details, BPAT license, gauge serial, and test date; registration requires gauge calibration proof within the last 12 months.
   - Site action: add TMR packet detail, gauge serial, gauge calibration, and incomplete-registration risk.

5. Fort Worth Backflow and Cross Connection Control
   - URL: https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/water/backflow
   - Useful facts: annual notices and report submission run through VEPO Envirotrax; BPATs must register with VEPO; testing is required before inspection for new, altered, replacement, and removal work; incomplete, missing, or inaccurate VEPO reports fail inspection; installation/permitting and annual enforcement have separate contact lines.
   - Site action: add Fort Worth permit/report-failure facts, contact routing, and priority sitemap coverage.

6. Euless Cross-Connection Control Program
   - URL: https://www.eulesstx.gov/departments/planning-and-economic-development/cross-connection-control-program
   - Useful facts: TrackMyBackflow handles notices and all submitted results; filing fee is published; Hazard ID and Site ID appear on customer communications and do not change.
   - Site action: no code patch needed this pass because the record already carries the TrackMyBackflow, filing-fee, Hazard ID, and Site ID pattern.

## Pattern Extracted

- Winning pages do not stop at "annual testing required." They expose the closeout evidence: portal name, due date, owner responsibility, tester credential gate, report packet, failed-test branch, and proof that the report was accepted.
- The strongest low-volume keywords are city plus portal plus action: `Aurora SpryBackflow`, `Anaheim SwiftComply photo`, `Hillsborough Backflow BMP`, `Austin WEIRS TMR`, `Fort Worth VEPO Envirotrax`.
- The codebase should keep adding hard operational facts to existing indexed pages before creating more generic variants.
