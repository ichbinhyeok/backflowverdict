## Quality Findings

1. The search blocker is not basic indexability. The site is indexable and already ranking around positions 5-12 for local pages. The problem is low CTR plus not enough high-intent query coverage.
2. Portal intent is the fastest path. The live data and public web examples both show users search for BSI, SwiftComply, WEIRS, tester portals, and submission workflows.
3. Guide pages need sharper SERP jobs. `backflow-reporting-portals` is already near page one but has 0 clicks. Its title/meta and internal links should make it look like a portal lookup page, not a generic article.
4. Provider pages can earn clicks, but they are mostly provider-name or local-service clicks. They should support the utility workflow, not become the core SEO bet.
5. The home page performance score is good enough to index, but LCP is slow. Optimizing fonts and the hero image can lift mobile quality and reduce bounce risk.
6. External URL validation found one definite broken source among the 50 cases: Lee County Utilities returned 404 for the stored official program URL. That registry source was replaced on 2026-07-03 with the official Lee County Utilities cross-connection policy PDF and BSI instruction PDF. Greeley and JEA errored under automated requests; many other 403s look like bot/WAF blocking and need manual review before treating them as broken.
