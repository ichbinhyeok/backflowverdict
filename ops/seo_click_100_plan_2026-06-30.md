# 100 Clicks/Day Search Plan

## North Star
BackflowPath should not compete as a generic "requirements" blog. It should become the utility-backed index for city-level backflow testing, approved tester lists, and reporting portal workflows.

## Current Signal
- 90-day GSC shows almost all demand is non-brand.
- Strongest pages are utility/provider pages, not broad guides.
- Quick-win queries cluster around city plus task:
  - `irvine backflow prevention`
  - `backflow preventer testing parker`
  - `scottsdale backflow prevention`
  - `backflow preventer aurora co`
  - `backflow testing thornton`
  - `backflow prevention for commercial sprinklers in englewood`

## Strategy
1. Stop wasting city intent on redirects.
   - Turn direct city aliases into indexable city landing pages.
   - Keep county or weak authority aliases as noindex bridges.

2. Make each city page a task router, not a thin doorway.
   - Requirements
   - Annual testing
   - Reporting portal
   - Approved tester route
   - Failed-test or repair path
   - Irrigation/fire-line paths when supported

3. Use state pages as internal-link hubs.
   - Every state guide should expose its city routes.
   - City pages route back down to utility pages and provider profiles.

4. Expand coverage next by city, not by generic articles.
   - Add more utility records only when they unlock city URLs and official tester or portal demand.
   - Priority markets: Colorado front range, Arizona metro cities, California utility districts, Florida county utilities, Texas suburbs.

5. Measure by query family.
   - city + backflow testing
   - city + backflow prevention
   - city + approved backflow testers
   - city + backflow reporting portal
   - city + backflow preventer repair/replacement

## Immediate Ship
- Convert 68 direct city aliases from 301 redirects to indexable city pages.
- Keep 4 bridge aliases noindex.
- Add city pages to sitemap and state-guide internal links.
- Create a city reindex URL file for Search Console requests.

## Why This Can Move Toward 100/day
The site already has scattered page-one/page-two positions with very low content footprint. The highest-leverage move is to expose the exact city terms Google is already testing, while preserving the utility-first source model that differentiates the site from generic provider directories.
